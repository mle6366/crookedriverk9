Day Camp

“Our Day Camp program combines intensive daytime training with the benefit of your dog returning home each evening. Dogs spend their day learning new skills, building confidence, practicing good habits, and receiving structured mental and physical enrichment. This approach allows owners to remain an active part of the training process while reinforcing progress at home, creating lasting results both at home and in real-world environments.

Basic Program

Complete

Intermediate Program

25 days Program length
$3,300 Investment

Best for: Owners who want everything in Essentials, plus intro to e-collar conditioning, recall, stay, and relax.

Skill Examples

  • All Essentials skills, refined and proofed
  • Cot stay — relaxation protocols for anxious or high-drive dogs
  • Introduction to e-collar conditioning (paves the way for off leash obedience)
  • Recall
  • Impulse control
  • Crate Manners

Next step: Graduates qualify for Elite.

Elite

Advanced Program

15 days Program length
$2,000 Investment

Best for: Dogs who have graduated from Complete and owners who need a higher level of obedience, control, and reliability under heavy distraction in new locations.

Skill Examples

  • Advanced obedience proofed in high-distraction environments
  • Training in new locations to generalize skills
  • Enhanced control and handler focus under pressure
  • Reliability when it matters most — busy parks, public spaces, and travel
  • Graduate-only intensive; builds on Complete, not a standalone start
